# Measured examples and limitations

These measurements describe individual local runs on an NVIDIA L40S. They are
examples, not guarantees for another training seed or Google Colab runtime.

| Example | Training steps | Training time | Observed behavior |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Walking baseline | 12,000 | 1,182 s | Passed the short forward-walking check |
| Walking reward variation | 12,000 | 923 s | Passed the short forward-walking check |
| Stationary hopping | 6,000 | 592 s | Three hops with 5.6–5.7 cm sole clearance, 0.20 s flight, and no falls |

The walking variation changes the sole-level reward. Compare it with its control
at the same commanded speed and training budget. A larger return after changing
a reward is not, by itself, evidence of better locomotion.

The hopping trials also checked two seconds of stance after each landing, with
no foot lifts observed. This establishes neither obstacle clearance nor
reliability across independently trained policies.

Both successful and unsuccessful training outcomes have been observed. A short
forward-walking check does not establish turning, terrain handling, or complete
course performance. Use the measurements and videos generated by your own run.

Timing excludes setup unless stated otherwise. Local execution is not validation
of actual Google Colab execution. Media metadata records the source settings,
measurements, playback information, and applicable limitations.
